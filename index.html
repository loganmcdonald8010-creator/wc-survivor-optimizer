Top 32 group picks — ranked by Edge
Edge = Advance% × P(not needed in the knockout)
Edge = how often the team is a usable group pick in an entry that wins every round — it advances and your deep teams are left free to run the table. Advance% = real chance to reach R32 (Opta). P(not needed) = share of simulated tournaments where the team isn’t deployed in any knockout round. So reliable-but-shallow teams rank high; deep teams (Spain, Germany, Brazil…) rank low because you’d rather save them. Edge can never exceed a team’s Advance%.
Burn = advance% × P(lose in R32) — the stricter "advances then immediately exits." Highlighted rows are the top recommended group picks.
